What Shapes Us

Our Core Values

Eight non-negotiable convictions that define our culture, guide our decisions, and shape the way we do life together.

01

Scripture Authority

We believe in the Holy Bible as the medium of God — the very Word speaking to mankind, as holy men of God spoke as they were moved by the Holy Spirit (2 Peter 1:21).

02

Holiness First

Holiness is our focus. Without holiness, no one will see God. We are called to restore the fear of God into the hearts of His people (Hebrews 12:14).

03

God's Creation & Redemption

We believe God created the world, placed man on earth with dominion, and — after man lost that dominion — sent Jesus Christ to restore it through His death and resurrection (Genesis 1:26–30, 2 Corinthians 5:18–21).

04

New Birth in Water & Spirit

We believe every person who hears and accepts God's Word must be baptised into Christ Jesus. Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God (John 3:5, Ephesians 5:26).

05

Water Baptism

We believe in water baptism as a symbol of one's death to sin and total trust in Jesus Christ, as well as a commitment to live faithfully to God (Romans 6:3–6).

06

The Holy Spirit

Jesus sent us the Helper — the Holy Spirit — to be with us forever, to help us, teach us, and comfort us in times of trials. He comes through laying of hands, impartation, or as the Spirit wills (Ephesians 5:26).

07

Unity in the Body of Christ

We express unity with all believers around the world, together as one body in Christ Jesus. We are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints (Ephesians 2:19, Romans 6:3–6).

08

The Trinity

We believe in the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit — the three agree in everything. This is the foundation of our faith and the source of all truth and power.
